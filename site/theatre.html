Meet the Maker: The Bhat Puppeteer Tradition

Kathputli is a sacred secular oral tradition preserved by the Bhat community of Marwar. Each doll is hand-hewn with simple adzes from local Rohida wood, adorned with bandhani tie-dye fabrics, and given voice by a reed mouth-whistle (*Boli*). We dedicate this virtual recreation to the living masters who continue this vibrant art form.
