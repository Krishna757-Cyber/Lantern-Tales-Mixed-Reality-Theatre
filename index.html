Act I Performance Arc

Five Beats of Light & Clay

Each hand shape is a line of dialogue. Step into the shoes of the Dalang (puppeteer) and perform the fable without uttering a single word.

Beat 1 • Kindle0:00 — 0:12

Awakening the Brass Lantern

Present an open palm. The wick catches fire, casting warm amber ripples over the textured parchment wall.

Beat 2 • The Beak0:12 — 0:28

The Origami Crow Awakens

Bring thumb and index together. A pointed beak forms on the wall. Hold for two heartbeats as paper perforations shiver.

Beat 3 • Hero Moment0:28 — 0:42

Flight Around the Room Corner

Spread your fingers like plumage. The bird detaches, bends physically around your room corner, and soars away.

Beat 4 • The Rising Sea0:42 — 1:05

Pebbles in the Dry Vessel

Clench your fist to manifest glowing stones. Each drop raises the water level drop by drop.

Beat 5 • The Duet1:05 — 1:30

Two Hands Across the Wall

A companion shadow hand enters from the opposite wall — whether live guest or ghost. Palms meet in passthrough.

Cultural Heritage

Living Traditions of Shadow

Wayang Kulit (Java & Bali)

The Dalang sits behind the cotton Kelir screen with an overhead oil flame, animating perforated buffalo hide.

Chhaya Natak (Ancient India)

Sanskrit illuminated theatre originating in the Natya Shastra, exploring philosophical epics through silhouettes.

Aesop's Fable (Perry 198)

The Crow and the Pitcher: perseverance and tool use passed down through oral tradition across cultures.
