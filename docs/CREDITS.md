# 📜 Cultural Lineage & Open Source Credits

## Cultural Stewardship & Oral Traditions
1. **Wayang Kulit (Java, Bali & Southeast Asia)**:
   - The ancient tradition of shadow puppetry performed by a master Dalang against a cotton screen (Kelir) illuminated by a coconut-oil lamp (Blencong).
   - Re-imagined in Lantern Tales as a digital homage with utmost respect; no sacred or religious deities are depicted.

2. **Chhaya Natak (छाया नाटक — India)**:
   - Gestural shadow theatre documented in Bharata Muni's *Natya Shastra* (c. 200 BCE – 200 CE).
   - Inspires the poetic Rang-Manch stage pact between light, screen, and human palm.

3. **Kathputli (कठपुतली — Rajasthan, India)**:
   - Dedication Card: *"In honour of the kathputli puppeteers of Rajasthan, whose fingers hold centuries of living rhythm and light."*
   - Preserved and performed by hereditary master puppeteers of the Bhat community (Nagaur, Jaipur, Churu).
   - Re-imagined in Lantern Tales as a digital homage with utmost respect; strictly secular folk pieces (Anarkali, Amar Singh Rathore, Ghodi aur Savar, Sapera, Mela Night); no sacred deities or scroll-narratives depicted.

4. **Aesopica (Perry Index 198 — The Crow and the Pitcher)**:
   - Public-domain world fable celebrating problem solving, patience, and tool use. Parallel parables occur in the Indian *Panchatantra* and Buddhist *Jataka* tales.

## Acoustic & Procedural Foley Lineage
- **Boli (घोंणा / बोली)**: Procedural Web Audio reed whistle synthesizer modeled after the authentic bamboo-and-reed mouth whistle used by Rajasthani puppeteers.
- **Dholak & Manjira**: Procedural rhythmic synthesis engine generating folk *Theka* rhythms without pre-recorded samples.
- **Ghungroo**: Dynamic metallic bell resonance foley triggered on dance steps.
